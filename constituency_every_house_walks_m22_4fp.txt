CONSTITUENCY EVERY-HOUSE WALKING MASTER PLAN
Starting Location: Saint Hilda's Road, Northenden, Manchester (M22 4FP)
Objective: Cover every residential street in the constituency via 30-minute walks
Total Estimated Walks: 60 Walks (30 Minutes Each | 15 Mins Out, 15 Mins Back)
Total Estimated Walking Distance: ~90 - 100 Miles (accounting for cul-de-sacs)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW & STRATEGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To systematically cover every single house in the constituency boundary:
1. Every walk is strictly designed as a 30-minute out-and-back or loop walk (~1.2 to 1.6 miles).
2. Side streets, dead-ends, and cul-de-sacs must be walked down and back to cover all house frontages.
3. Use this checklist document to track your progress by marking [X] as you complete each route.
